# Technical Steering Committee

The TSC is the technical governing body of OpenDCKit and is responsible for all technical
oversight of the project, as set out in the Technical Charter. Its meetings are open to the
public.

This file is the authoritative roster. The organization's
[CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/opendckit/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) explains
how TSC voting membership is determined.

> **Draft.** The founding group needs to confirm this roster, with a GitHub username for each
> member, before the charter is adopted.

## Chair

TBD. The chair presides over TSC meetings and serves until resignation or replacement by the
TSC. The chair, or another TSC member the TSC designates, is the primary contact between the
project and LF Networking.

## Meetings

Weekly on Mondays at 07:00 America/Los_Angeles. Agendas and minutes are on the
[OpenDCKit wiki](https://lf-networking.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OpenDCKit/pages/1534820367).

## Changes

Each change to this roster is a pull request that links to the election result in
[`elections/`](elections/), or to the TSC minutes recording a resignation or removal.
