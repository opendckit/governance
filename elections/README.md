# TSC Elections

After inception, TSC seats are filled by election of the Maintainers, as described in the
organization's [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/opendckit/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
The Linux Foundation administers each election as a neutral party. The Maintainers nominate
and vote.

Each election is recorded here in its own file, named for the year and month the vote closed
(for example `2027-06-tsc.md`), with:

- the dates of the nomination period and the vote
- the seats up for election
- the nominees
- the result, and the members seated

The roster in [`TSC.md`](../TSC.md) is updated in the same pull request.

No elections have been held yet. The initial TSC members were appointed at inception.
